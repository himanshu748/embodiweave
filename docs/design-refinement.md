# Lab interface refinement

Reference reviewed: [Manu Arora's Aceternity studio](https://www.aceternity.com/), 16 October 2026. The reference informed framed page hierarchy, a quieter navigation surface, restrained depth on controls, and clear cards that lead to real interactions. Its artwork, copy and components were not copied.

The project's custom-design guide and existing robotics identity remain the visual authority: engineering paper, graphite, safety orange, Space Grotesk and IBM Plex. The signature visual remains the real recorded paired simulator episode. The current 2D confirmation is separate from that historical replay and from the physics adaptation study.

The landing hierarchy is hook, why graph evidence matters, measured confirmation, then direct judge routes. On mobile, this reading order is explicit. Cards lead to the confirmed checkpoint, bounded live training and the narrated film. The separate physics page exposes limits, rejected attempts and confirmation status rather than hiding uncertainty behind animation.

Verification includes widths 360, 768 and 1440, reduced-motion final replay state, usable links and visible query states. No generated robot imagery was introduced.
