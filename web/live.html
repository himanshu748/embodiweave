How this works

The agent discovers tools through MCP, reads FalkorDB, and proposes its own origin. The guardrail can veto it. If the model provider is unavailable, a labelled deterministic MCP fallback can act on an explicitly requested training task. Watch the simulator below.

Up to 6 model turns, 10 tool calls and 2 minutes. One investigation runs at a time.

    What counts as a pass?

    The target test requires at least 80% success and at most 10% collisions. Three tests for earlier tasks must also pass. Finishing training saves a checkpoint even when it fails these checks.

    A validation pass does not automatically replace the current demo policy. The saved results show each threshold and measurement.
